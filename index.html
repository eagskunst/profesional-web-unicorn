About

With 8 years of experience in software development and a B.S. in Computer Science, I've worked on diverse projects ranging from enterprise systems to mobile applications.

  • A full feature in a week? Done it.
  • A regression crash that makes no sense? Fixed it.
  • Weird animations and components without libraries? Built them.
  • Compile native libraries? Dockerized it.
  • Full DB migration? Yeah, but not on Fridays.

I take pride and ownership of what I do. Regardless of no users or millions of it, I go all in from planning the tasks to deployment and monitoring.

While my main focus is mobile, my curiosity has driven me to explore many fields of software engineering: databases, backends, data engineering, you name it.

Currently exploring AI integrations and development tools. When not coding, you'll find me playing video games, reading, and writing.
